# 📏 Evaluation

Measuring AI model performance, quality, and behavior through metrics, benchmarks, and testing methodologies.

## What is AI Evaluation?

AI evaluation is the process of assessing how well a model performs on specific tasks and whether it meets quality, safety, and reliability standards. Unlike traditional software testing, AI evaluation must account for probabilistic outputs, subjective quality, and potential harms.

Evaluation includes:
- **Performance Metrics:** Quantitative measures like accuracy, perplexity, BLEU scores
- **Benchmarks:** Standardized tests for comparing models
- **Human Evaluation:** Subjective assessment by human raters
- **Safety Testing:** Identifying harmful outputs, biases, and vulnerabilities
- **Red Teaming:** Adversarial testing to find model weaknesses

## Terms in This Category

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [Perplexity](../terms/perplexity/) | A core metric measuring how "surprised" a language model is by the next word; lower is better. |
| [Hallucination](../terms/hallucination/) | When an AI confidently generates factually incorrect or fabricated information with no basis in reality. |

## Why Evaluation Matters

Without proper evaluation, organizations cannot:
- Compare different AI models objectively
- Ensure models meet quality standards before deployment
- Detect harmful biases or unsafe behaviors
- Track performance improvements over time
- Make data-driven decisions about model selection

Robust evaluation is essential for responsible AI deployment and continuous improvement.
