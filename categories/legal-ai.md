# ⚖️ Legal AI

AI terminology, regulatory frameworks, and legal principles governing the development, deployment, and accountability of artificial intelligence systems. This category covers the intersection of AI technology, law, ethics, and compliance.

## Terms in This Category

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [AI Act](/ai-dictionary/terms/ai-act/) | The world's first comprehensive legal framework regulating artificial intelligence, classifying AI systems by risk level and imposing strict obligations on high-risk applications. |
| [AI Liability](/ai-dictionary/terms/ai-liability/) | The legal frameworks and principles determining who is legally responsible and financially liable when an AI system causes harm, damage, or loss. |
| [Algorithmic Accountability](/ai-dictionary/terms/algorithmic-accountability/) | The principle requiring organizations to take responsibility for the outcomes, impacts, and potential harms caused by their automated decision-making systems. |
| [Algorithmic Audit](/ai-dictionary/terms/algorithmic-audit/) | A systematic, independent evaluation of an AI system to assess its compliance with legal standards, ethical guidelines, and performance metrics, focusing on fairness and safety. |
| [Automated Decision-Making (ADM)](/ai-dictionary/terms/automated-decision-making/) | The process of making decisions by automated means without human intervention, triggering specific legal rights and protections for individuals under data protection laws. |
| [eDiscovery (Electronic Discovery)](/ai-dictionary/terms/ediscovery/) | The process of identifying, collecting, and producing electronically stored information in response to legal requests, heavily augmented by AI for sorting and analysis. |
| [High-Risk AI System](/ai-dictionary/terms/high-risk-ai-system/) | A legal classification for AI systems posing significant threats to health, safety, or fundamental rights, subjecting them to strict compliance obligations before market entry. |
| [Impact Assessment (Algorithmic)](/ai-dictionary/terms/impact-assessment/) | A proactive, structured process used to identify, evaluate, and mitigate the potential risks and societal impacts of an AI system before it is deployed. |
| [Regulatory Sandbox](/ai-dictionary/terms/regulatory-sandbox/) | A controlled, supervised environment established by regulators where AI developers can test innovative systems under real-world conditions with temporary regulatory flexibility. |
| [Right to Explanation](/ai-dictionary/terms/right-to-explanation/) | A legal principle granting individuals the right to receive a meaningful explanation for any significant decision made about them by an automated system. |
| [Systemic Risk](/ai-dictionary/terms/systemic-risk/) | The potential for highly capable AI models to cause widespread, cascading harm across society or the economy, triggering enhanced regulatory oversight. |
| [Trustworthy AI](/ai-dictionary/terms/trustworthy-ai/) | A comprehensive framework for developing AI systems that are lawful, ethical, and robust, ensuring they respect fundamental rights and societal values. |
